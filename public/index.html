📐 Entity Relationship (ER) Model & Database Schema

3NF / BCNF Normalized

1. Relational Entities & Structural Cardinalities

  • DEPARTMENTS (1) ────< (M) CATEGORIES: One department governs multiple specific grievance categories with individual SLAs.
  • CITIZENS (1) ────< (M) GRIEVANCES: A citizen can register multiple complaints; each complaint belongs strictly to one citizen.
  • DEPARTMENTS (1) ────< (M) OFFICERS: Officers belong to a specific department.
  • OFFICERS (1) ────< (M) OFFICERS (Self-Join): Hierarchical supervisory tree where an officer reports to a designated supervisor.
  • OFFICERS (1) ────< (M) GRIEVANCES: An officer is assigned to investigate and resolve grievances.
  • GRIEVANCES (1) ────< (M) GRIEVANCE_TIMELINE: Complete immutable audit log of status changes and action taken notes.
  • GRIEVANCES (1) ────< (1) GRIEVANCE_FEEDBACK: Exactly one feedback & star rating per resolved grievance (enforced via UNIQUE(grievance_id)).
  • GRIEVANCES (1) ────< (M) ESCALATIONS: Overdue or appealed grievances escalate through hierarchical tiers.

2. Normalization Breakdown

First Normal Form (1NF): All attributes contain atomic values (single values only). Each table has a unique primary key with no repeating groups.
Second Normal Form (2NF): Achieved 1NF, and all non-key attributes are fully functionally dependent on the entire primary key (no partial dependencies).
Third Normal Form (3NF): Achieved 2NF, and no transitive dependencies exist (i.e. non-prime attributes do not depend on other non-prime attributes). Department names and category SLA days are isolated into dedicated relation tables.
